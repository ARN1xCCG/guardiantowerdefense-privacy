🛡️ Privacy Policy

Important: Please read our Privacy Policy to understand how we collect, use, and protect your data.

Welcome to Guardian Tower Defense! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Gameplay Data: Game progress, scores, achievements
  • Analytics Data: How you interact with the game, performance metrics
  • Advertising ID: For personalized ads (you can reset this in your device settings)

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve game features
  • Save your game progress
  • Show relevant advertisements
  • Analyze game performance and fix bugs
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

3. Third-Party Services

Our game uses third-party services that may collect information:

  • Appodeal: Ad mediation service
  • Google Play Services: For achievements and leaderboards
  • Analytics Services: To understand game usage

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

4. Advertising

We show ads to keep the game free. Our advertising partners may collect data to show you relevant ads. You can opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our game is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of certain data processing

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the game.
